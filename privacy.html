Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

Iris is a private personal assistant used only by its owner. This page explains what Iris does with Google data.

What Iris accesses

Iris requests a single Google permission: read-only access to its owner's Google Calendar (calendar.readonly). It reads event titles and times so it can list the day's schedule in the owner's daily check-ins.

Iris does not access email, files, contacts or any other Google data, and it cannot create, change or delete calendar events.

Where the data goes

Calendar data is read on the owner's own computer and written only into the owner's private notes on that computer. It is not sold, shared, published or sent to anyone else, and it is not used for advertising.

Who uses Iris

Only the owner. Iris is not offered to the public and collects no data about anyone else.

Removing access

The owner can remove Iris's access at any time from their Google Account under Security, "Your connections to third-party apps and services". Doing so stops all calendar access immediately.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Iris's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
