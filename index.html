Iris

A private personal assistant.

Iris is a personal assistant used by one person, its owner. It helps them keep track of their day: their schedule, their to-do list and a short morning and evening check-in.

What Iris does with Google

Iris asks Google for one permission only: to read its owner's Google Calendar. It uses that to list the day's events in the owner's check-ins. Iris cannot create, change or delete calendar events, and it has no access to email, files or contacts.

Who can use it

Iris is not a public service. It is not offered to anyone else and there is nothing to sign up for.
